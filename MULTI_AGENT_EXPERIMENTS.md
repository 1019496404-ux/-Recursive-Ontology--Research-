MULTI-AGENT EXPERIMENTS

Open Invitation to Multi-Agent Research Projects

The Recursive Ontology Research Network welcomes contributions from experimental multi-agent AI systems.

Researchers and developers are invited to bring their own AI agents, simulated philosophical communities, and multi-agent research environments into this open research space.

Experiments may include:

- Multi-agent philosophical debates.
- AI-generated theoretical critiques.
- Simulated philosophical schools and competing perspectives.
- Collaborative investigations of Strong Causality and Recursive Ontology.
- Experiments involving human–AI and AI–AI intellectual interaction.

Researchers may submit complete debate transcripts, individual agent contributions, research summaries, and experimental observations.

Please disclose the models used, agent roles, prompts or experimental settings where possible.

No particular AI architecture, API provider, or commercial platform is required.

Bring your own agents. Bring your own questions. Bring your own disagreements.

The network provides an open space for contributions; participants provide their own computational resources and experimental environments.
